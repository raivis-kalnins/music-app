# Music 63 v2.3 - free handwriting photo scan

Handwritten melody photos can now be converted without an API key.

## Free local workflow

1. Open **Music Editor** and choose the destination part/instrument.
2. Under **Handwritten import**, choose **Load handwriting photo**.
3. Rotate the photo if needed and press **Prepare / enhance image**.
4. Keep **Reader -> Free local scan (no API)**.
5. Press **Free scan to notes**.
6. Review the draft, then choose **Replace current part**, **Add to current part**, or **Create new imported part**.
7. Correct any missed or uncertain notes in the normal editor.

The free reader runs in the browser. The photo is not uploaded to an AI service. It detects five-line staff systems, removes staff lines, finds handwritten noteheads, maps them to treble-clef pitches, estimates bar/beat positions, and creates a normal editable Music 63 note draft.

## Best results

- Use a sharp photo with the page filling most of the frame.
- Keep staff lines roughly horizontal. Use Rotate and Prepare if needed.
- The free reader is designed for one treble-clef melody line, such as clarinet, sax, piano right hand or a simple vocal melody.
- Rhythm is estimated from spacing and should be reviewed after import.
- Handwritten chord letters are not read by the local scanner. Add them manually, or switch to the optional AI reader if it is configured.

## Optional AI reader

The previous server-side image reader is still available as **Optional AI high accuracy**. It is no longer required for handwritten melody import.
