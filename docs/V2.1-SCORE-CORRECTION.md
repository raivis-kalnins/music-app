# Music 63 v2.1 - score correction and classic beaming

## Classic 4/4 placement

The clef and time signature now sit on the staff in the normal engraving area before bar 1. The meter is drawn as stacked figures (for example 4 over 4) directly after the treble clef instead of looking like ordinary inline text.

## Correct a dense or wrong passage

The Music Editor left panel now has **Passage correction**.

1. Click any note in the bar you want to fix. That bar becomes highlighted.
2. Choose a **Clean density** level:
   - up to 4 notes per beat (1/16 detail)
   - up to 2 notes per beat (1/8, recommended for normal melody cleanup)
   - up to 1 note per beat (simple melody)
3. Use one of the correction actions:
   - **Snap timing** - aligns note starts and lengths to the selected grid.
   - **Clean extra notes** - removes competing notes in the same timing slot, preferring the more confident/stronger note and a smoother melodic line.
   - **Join repeated notes** - merges adjacent repeated pitches into one longer note.
   - **Clear this bar** - removes only the active bar.
4. Use **Undo** at any time if the automatic cleanup removed something you wanted to keep.

The active bar is lightly highlighted on the score so it is clear which passage will be changed.

## Faster single-note correction

Selecting a note now gives quick controls above the score for:

- move earlier / later by the current editing grid;
- make the note shorter / longer;
- move pitch down / up by semitone;
- delete the note.

The right inspector still allows exact bar, beat, duration, velocity and MIDI pitch entry.

## Eighth / sixteenth-note beams

Short notes now beam more reliably, including notes imported from audio whose durations are not perfectly exact.

Two beam modes are available:

- **Classic by beat** - standard grouping by beat (recommended for normal notation).
- **Continuous in bar** - keeps a run of consecutive eighth/sixteenth notes connected across the bar until a rest or timing gap breaks the phrase.

Stem direction is chosen for the beam group, and 16th/32nd notes receive secondary/tertiary beams where needed.
