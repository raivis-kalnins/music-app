# Music 63 v2.5 - deeper local handwriting scan

The handwriting photo reader remains free and local. It does not require an OpenAI key or any other API key.

## What changed

- New **Scan depth** selector: Deep, Sensitive, Balanced.
- **Deep** is recommended and is the default.
- Deep mode runs several local threshold passes at a higher working resolution.
- The prepared image keeps a separate colour analysis copy. When staff lines are strongly coloured (for example blue manuscript paper) the scanner can suppress that line colour and retain darker handwriting.
- Candidate noteheads are merged across passes and filtered for likely stem/flag fragments and chord/text marks.
- Strong ending barlines are used to ignore writing that appears after the music on a staff system.
- Deep mode uses a 1/16-beat placement grid to avoid collapsing close handwritten notes.

## Current limits

This is heuristic optical music recognition running entirely in the browser. It works best with one treble-clef melody line. Handwritten chord names, rests, exact beaming/flags, accidentals, ties/slurs, and exact rhythmic values can still require manual correction after import.
