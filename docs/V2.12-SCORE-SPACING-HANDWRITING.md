# Music 63 v2.12 – score spacing and handwriting pitch cleanup

This update fixes several notation/OMR presentation issues visible after importing the photographed F-major example.

## Handwriting pitch mapping

The local scanner now carries the detected staff step into the editable score. Before importing, Music 63 recalculates the MIDI pitch from that staff step and the final written key. This prevents a note that was detected on the B staff position from being rendered on a different line/space after the key is changed or auto-detected.

Major and minor key signatures are both respected. F major and D minor, for example, both apply B-flat automatically.

## False dots

Small detached dots close to a stronger notehead are rejected when they look like staccato/articulation marks. Repeat-sign dots next to a strong barline remain filtered as before.

## Accidentals

Key-signature symbols use a larger, centered music glyph. Repeated identical accidentals in the same measure are not redrawn unnecessarily. Accidentals are shown again only when the written alteration actually changes.

## Engraving spacing

Notes use a fixed inner margin inside each measure instead of being pushed against barlines by duration-based positioning. Note-name labels are centered under their noteheads. The score header also reserves more space between clef, key signature, time signature and the first barline.

## Inspector/UI

The score-layout controls are forced to the inspector width, the chord-entry row no longer creates horizontal scrolling, and the lyrics controls use a lighter compact style.
